L'emplacement

The L'Autre Bord neighbourhood, between the beach and the harbour

The homes are in L'Autre Bord, in the commune of Le Moule, in the heart of the L'Autre Bord sports park. The beach is a two-minute walk; the fishing harbour, where you can buy the catch of the day, is right next door. The town centre and its shops are a few minutes away, so you can enjoy your stay without depending on a car.
